If something breaks, confuses you, or looks wrong, use [**Report an issue**](/report) to send us a short description.

## What to include

- A **short title** that summarizes the problem
- **What happened** — what you expected vs what you saw
- Optional **steps to reproduce** if you can

If you are signed in, we attach your account email automatically. If you are signed out, you can optionally add an email for follow-up.

## From an error screen

When compilation fails, a page errors, or citing a DOI fails, look for **Report this issue**. That link opens the report form with the current page prefilled so we know where you were.

## Privacy

Reports are stored in our internal tracker for the Paplyn team. Do not paste secrets (API keys, passwords) into the form.
